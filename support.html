How to play

A factory pushes blocks up out of five vents. Bobo stands on the platform at the top. Tap blocks to pop them before any stack reaches him. Popping again quickly builds a combo, up to five times. Bombs clear the area around them and set off other bombs; spiky blocks ignore taps and can only be cleared by a blast.

I bought Remove Ads + Pro but the ads are back

Open the pause screen or the main menu and tap RESTORE. That asks the App Store or Google Play what you own and unlocks it again. This is the right step after reinstalling or moving to a new device — we hold no record of your purchase ourselves, the store does.

The RESTORE and REMOVE ADS buttons are missing

They hide when the store cannot be reached, and when you already own Pro. If you are online and still do not see them, close the game completely and reopen it.

I watched an ad but did not get the rescue

The rescue is only granted when the ad finishes. Closing it early, or losing connection part way through, earns nothing — that is deliberate, not a bug. Each run offers the rescue once.

Changing your ad consent

In the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, open the pause screen and tap Privacy options. On iOS, tracking permission lives in the system Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device. Deleting the game deletes it. We hold nothing to delete on our side; see the privacy policy.
